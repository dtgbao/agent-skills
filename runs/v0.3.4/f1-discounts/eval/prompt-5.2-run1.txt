Cited criterion text:
- 5.1: WHEN the Handler gets `PUT /carts/<id>/code` with body `{"code": <string>}` and the attach succeeds, THE Handler SHALL answer status 200 with `subtotal_cents`, `discount_cents`, and `amount_due_cents`.
- 5.2: IF the attach is refused, THEN THE Handler SHALL answer status 422 with body `{"error": <reason>}`.
- 5.3: IF the body of `PUT /carts/<id>/code` lacks `code` or `code` is not a string, THEN THE Handler SHALL answer status 400 with body `{"error": "invalid_body"}`.
- 5.4: WHEN the Handler gets `DELETE /carts/<id>/code`, THE Handler SHALL answer status 200 with `subtotal_cents`, `discount_cents`, and `amount_due_cents`.
- 5.5: WHEN the Handler gets `GET /carts/<id>`, THE Handler SHALL add `code`, `discount_cents`, and `amount_due_cents` to the existing body, with `code` null when the Cart has no Attached_Code.
- 5.6: IF `ctx["today"]` is missing or not a `datetime.date`, or `ctx["code_book"]` is missing, on a request to a route in Requirement 5, THEN THE Handler SHALL answer status 500 with body `{"error": "bad_context"}` before any body check.
- 5.7: WHEN the Handler answers status 500 with `bad_context`, THE Handler SHALL leave every Cart's lines and Attached_Code unchanged.

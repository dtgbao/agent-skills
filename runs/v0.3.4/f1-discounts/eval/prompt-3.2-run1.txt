Cited criterion text:
- 2.1: WHEN the Shopper attaches a Code that is in the Code_Book, Current_Date is on or before Valid_Until, and the Subtotal is at least Min_Subtotal, THE Cart SHALL make that Discount_Code its Attached_Code.
- 2.2: IF the Code is not in the Code_Book, THEN THE Cart SHALL refuse the attach with reason `code_not_found`.
- 2.3: IF Current_Date is after Valid_Until, THEN THE Cart SHALL refuse the attach with reason `code_expired`.
- 2.4: IF the Subtotal is less than Min_Subtotal, THEN THE Cart SHALL refuse the attach with reason `minimum_not_met`.
- 2.5: IF the Cart already has an Attached_Code, THEN THE Cart SHALL refuse the attach with reason `code_already_attached`.
- 2.6: WHEN the Cart refuses an attach, THE Cart SHALL keep its Attached_Code as it was before the attach.
- 2.7: IF more than one refusal condition holds, THEN THE Cart SHALL refuse with the first reason in the order `code_not_found`, `code_already_attached`, `code_expired`, `minimum_not_met`.
- 3.1: WHEN the Shopper detaches the code from a Cart, THE Cart SHALL have no Attached_Code.
- 3.2: IF the Cart has no Attached_Code when the Shopper detaches, THEN THE Cart SHALL finish without an error.
- 4.4: THE Cart SHALL report Amount_Due = Subtotal − Discount_Amount cents.

Cited criterion text:
- 1.1: WHEN the Operator creates a Discount_Code whose fields are all in range, THE Code_Book SHALL hold that Discount_Code under its Code.
- 1.2: IF the Kind is `percent` and the Amount is outside 1–50, THEN THE Code_Book SHALL refuse the Discount_Code and stay unchanged.
- 1.3: IF the Kind is `fixed` and the Amount is outside 1–1,000,000, THEN THE Code_Book SHALL refuse the Discount_Code and stay unchanged.
- 1.4: IF the Code does not match the Code format, THEN THE Code_Book SHALL refuse the Discount_Code and stay unchanged.
- 1.5: IF the Min_Subtotal is outside 0–1,000,000, THEN THE Code_Book SHALL refuse the Discount_Code and stay unchanged.
- 1.6: IF the Code_Book already holds a Discount_Code with the same Code, THEN THE Code_Book SHALL refuse the new Discount_Code and keep the old one.
- 1.7: IF the Kind is not `percent` or `fixed`, or the Amount is not an integer, THEN THE Code_Book SHALL refuse the Discount_Code and stay unchanged.
- 4.1: WHILE the Attached_Code has Kind `percent`, THE Cart SHALL report Discount_Amount = floor(Subtotal × Amount / 100) cents.
- 4.2: WHILE the Attached_Code has Kind `fixed`, THE Cart SHALL report Discount_Amount = min(Amount, Subtotal) cents.
- 4.3: WHILE the Cart has no Attached_Code, THE Cart SHALL report Discount_Amount = 0 cents.
- 4.5: WHILE the Subtotal is less than the Attached_Code's Min_Subtotal, THE Cart SHALL report Discount_Amount = 0 cents, overriding 4.1 and 4.2.
- 4.6: WHILE Current_Date is after the Attached_Code's Valid_Until, THE Cart SHALL report Discount_Amount = 0 cents, overriding 4.1 and 4.2.
